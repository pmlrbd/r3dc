01 — Abstract

Three Challenges,
One Architecture

Depth completion is fundamental to 3D perception, yet practical deployment is hindered by three challenges: an absence of calibrated per-pixel confidence, poor cross-domain generalization, and benchmarks that evaluate accuracy while ignoring the trustworthiness of uncertainty estimates. We introduce R³DC, an end-to-end Reveal-to-Revise framework that jointly predicts dense metric depth, per-pixel reliability, and aleatoric uncertainty. The architecture integrates a dual-stream encoder with geometry-adaptive deformable convolutions, hierarchical cross-modal attention, and CSPN++ refinement explicitly gated by learned reliability. To rigorously assess these confidence estimates, we propose RADI (Reliability-Aware Depth Index), a novel evaluation framework measuring reliability-error correlation (REC), revision benefit score (RBS), and calibration error (CAL). Across four heterogeneous benchmarks, R³DC achieves highly competitive accuracy while requiring 10 to 170× fewer parameters than existing baselines.

i
⚠ No Per-Pixel Confidence
Downstream modules like motion planning must know where to trust depth estimates. Textureless walls, reflective surfaces, and occlusion boundaries receive identical treatment. No current method outputs calibrated, per-pixel reliability jointly with depth.
ii
🌐 Cross-Domain Fragility
Ground-level LiDAR models generalize poorly to aerial cameras, drone video, or indoor RGB-D. No prior work validates a single domain-agnostic architecture across all four modalities using a unified training protocol.
iii
📏 Reliability Evaluation Gap
Standard metrics like RMSE and δ₁ quantify average accuracy but not trustworthiness. They can favor models that perform well on easy pixels while remaining dangerously overconfident in hard, ambiguous regions.

02b — Training Objective

Seven-Term Composite Loss

Each term contributes non-redundantly. Weights fixed across all four datasets after grid search on KITTI.

λ=1.00
L₁ · SILog (Scale-Invariant Log)
Cross-domain transfer. Log-space variance minus mean² removes scale bias. Critical when absolute depth offsets vary between sensors.
λ=0.60
L₂ · Focal-BerHu (γ=2)
Hard-example mining via focal weights (1−e^−|e|)². Up-weights difficult pixels (occlusion boundaries, distant objects) without hard thresholding.
λ=0.10
L₃ · Virtual Normal Loss (VNL)
Samples random point triplets and penalizes back-facing surface normals. Enforces global 3D plane consistency without camera intrinsics.
λ=0.05
L₄ · Depth-Normal Consistency (DNC)
Penalizes depth maps with misaligned local gradients and surface normals. Prevents asymmetric discontinuity artifacts from learning-based methods.
λ=0.20
L₅ · SSIM (window=7)
Structural similarity preserving edges and local contrast. Complements pixel-wise losses by capturing structural fidelity at a region level.
λ=0.15
L₆ · Sparse Anchor
Forces exact reproduction of known sparse measurements. Prevents metric-scale drift at extreme sparsity (0.5–2%). Acts as hard ground-truth anchoring.
λ=0.05
L₇ · Laplace Uncertainty-NLL
Self-supervised uncertainty calibration. Penalizes confident wrong predictions; prevents σ→0 collapse. No ground-truth uncertainty labels required.
λ=0.10
L₈ · Auxiliary Deep Supervision
Focal-BerHu at ½ and ¼ resolution exits. Propagates gradients to early decoder stages, stabilizing training across diverse depth ranges.

04 — RADI

Reliability-Aware
Depth Index

Standard metrics conflate accuracy and calibration. RADI is the first structured metric for evaluating depth completion reliability through three independent sub-scores.

REC
Component 1
Reliability–Error Correlation
Spearman rank correlation between predicted reliability R̂ and negative absolute error −|d̂−d|. Evaluated across four spatial regions: all, edge, textureless, and far-depth. Robust to outliers via rank-invariant formulation. A positive ρ confirms reliability predicts low error.
NYU: ρ = +0.430–0.444 *** (p<0.001)
RBS
Component 2
Revision Benefit Score
Measures whether reliability-gated CSPN++ revision genuinely improves coarse depth D₀. Computed as % RMSE improvement from D₀→D₁ per region. Validates that the architecture actually leverages predicted confidence — high REC alone is insufficient.
RBS > 62% in all regions
CAL
Component 3
Calibration Error (ECE-style)
Reliability values binned (B=15 equal-width bins). Measures if a claimed 80% confidence corresponds to ~80% accuracy (τ=0.10 tolerance). ECE→0 = perfect calibration; random uniform predictor yields ECE≈0.25.
ECE = 0.031 (vs 0.25 random)

06 — Limitations & Future Work

Honest Assessment

🛰
Synthesized Aerial Ground Truth
VisDrone and Drone-Videos evaluations rely on a physics-informed aerial prior rather than real LiDAR. Enables controlled benchmarking but limits direct comparability to real-sensor data.
⏱
No Temporal Consistency
The current approach processes frames independently. Online reliability adaptation or temporal smoothing could substantially improve predictions for dynamic video sequences.
📦
Small Dataset Scalability
Lightweight R³DC variants perform well on small datasets (516–8,168 samples). Testing on larger, diverse aerial datasets is required to fully assess scalability and real-world deployment.
🔄
Semi-Supervised Potential
Semi or self-supervised training on unlabeled frames offers a promising path to enhance robustness and generalization beyond the current supervised training protocol.
